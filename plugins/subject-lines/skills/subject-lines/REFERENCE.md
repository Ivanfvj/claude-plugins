## Findings

**Length**
- Belkins: 2–4 words 46% opens; 1 word 38%; 10 words 34%.
- Gong: open rates fall as lines get longer; 1–4 words best.
- Hunter: "5-6 words is the sweet spot for average reply rates."
- Saleshandy: 5–7 words highest opens (bucket values not published in text).
- Backlinko: 36–50 characters best response rate.
- Rework's verdict: the consistent message is short and specific; the data cannot separate 2 words from 5.

**Personalization**
- Belkins: personalized 46% vs 35% opens; replies 3% vs 7%.
- Hunter: one custom attribute 35.4%, two 40.2% opens.
- Backlinko: personalized subject lines +30.5% responses.
- Sahni et al. 2018: name in the subject raised opens (~9% → ~11%) and leads (~30%), cut unsubscribes 17%. Defau & Zauner 2023 did not reproduce the first-name effect.

**Specificity**
- Instantly: subject lines referencing "a specific problem, outcome, or situation relevant to the prospect's world get opened"; generic ones get ignored.
- Backlinko contrasts a specific line with a generic "More Leads".

**What hurts**
- Gong: buzzwords and numbers cut opens by up to 17.9%; social proof works in the body, not the subject.
- Belkins: jargon, generic greetings and urgency ("now", "ASAP") sit below 36% opens.
- Hunter: "Quick question" lines 2.5% replies / 28.7% opens vs 2.9% / 32.9% for others.

**Unsettled**
- Questions: Belkins ranks them top (46%, listed as 44% elsewhere on the same page); Gong advises avoiding them; Hunter's "Quick question" data underperforms.
- Casing: Belkins ALL CAPS 30%, title and lowercase 29%, first-letter-only 25%; Rework: "A gap of a few points, on sample sizes nobody reported, isn't a finding."

## Rules from primary policy

- [Gmail sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126): no "Re:" or "Fwd:" unless the message is a real reply or forward; subject lines "should accurately represent the sender identity and message content"; "Google doesn't track open rates."
- [FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business): the subject line must accurately reflect the message content.

## Why reply rate, not opens

Apple Mail Privacy Protection (June 2021) preloads tracking pixels; Litmus put Apple clients at 62.26% of tracked opens in July 2026 (via Rework). Open-rate findings above still show direction, but tests are judged on replies.
